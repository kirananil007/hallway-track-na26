## Before you fly

- **Pick your pass.** The All-Access pass includes Monday's CNCF-hosted co-located events;
  the KubeCon + CloudNativeCon-only pass doesn't (it does include Monday's Project Lightning
  Talks). See the [Monday guide](#/monday).
- **Plan, then export.** Build your plan here, then use *Export to calendar* so your schedule
  works offline. Conference Wi-Fi is often slow.
- **Mark backups.** Popular rooms fill up and seating is first come, first served.
  A backup in the same time slot saves you a scramble.
- **Pack layers.** It's November in Salt Lake City: check the forecast before you pack.
- **Salt Lake City is about 4,200 ft (1,300 m) above sea level.** Drink more water than usual.

## Getting there and around

- **Venue:** Salt Palace Convention Center, 100 S West Temple, Salt Lake City, UT 84101.
  Some sessions are in the Hyatt Regency next door; the room name on each session says which.
- **From the airport:** the TRAX Green Line light rail runs to downtown in about 20 minutes.
  See [Visit Salt Lake's transportation page](https://visitsaltlake.com/salt-palace-convention-center/attend/transportation-information)
  for current fares.
- **Downtown:** TRAX rides within the downtown Free Fare Zone are free. Arena and Temple Square
  stations are about half a block from the convention center.
- **Time zone:** Mountain Time. Daylight saving ends on Nov 1, so the whole week is
  MST (UTC−7). The times in this app are already in MST, or tap *My time*.

## Badge pick-up

Registration and badge pick-up open on **Sunday, Nov 8**. Current hours and location are in
the official schedule: [see them in Browse](#/browse?day=all&q=badge&other=1).
Picking up your badge on Sunday or early Monday avoids the Tuesday-morning queue.

## During the conference

- **Keynotes** are Tuesday to Thursday mornings in the main hall.
- **The hallway track is real.** Some of the best conversations happen between sessions,
  at the Project Pavilion and in the Solutions Showcase. Leave gaps in your plan.
- **Talks are usually recorded**, so prioritize sessions where you want to ask questions,
  and hands-on ones like tutorials and Contribfests.
- **Evenings:** check [Side events](#/side-events) for community meetups and happy hours.
